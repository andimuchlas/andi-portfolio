Andi Muchlas Ramadani (Andi Dev) — Software Developer Profile & Biography

Software Engineer specializing in Backend Systems, AI/ML Pipelines, and Real-Time Systems

Andi Muchlas Ramadani (known in the developer community as Andi Dev or Andi Developer) is a Software Engineer with 3+ years of experience based in Bandung, Indonesia. He specializes in designing resilient backend architectures, spatial query and routing engines, AI model orchestration pipelines, and interactive Unity simulations.

Core Engineering Philosophy

"Build systems that work. Not systems that impress."

Focused on first-principles design, performance optimization, and low-dependency engineering. Highly proficient in Go, TypeScript, Python, C#, PostGIS, OSRM, Redis, NATS, and Kubernetes.

Education

Bachelor of Computer Science (S.Kom.) from Bandung National Institute of Technology (ITENAS) with a GPA of 3.46. Served as Laboratory Teaching Assistant mentoring 150+ students in Game Programming, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), and Database Programming labs.
