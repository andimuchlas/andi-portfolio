1. RAJADEREK — Real-Time Spatial Routing & Logistics Platform

Role: Backend & Spatial Engineer | Stack: Go, TypeScript, Python (Scrapy), Typesense, OSRM, Uber H3, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, gRPC, Redis, NATS, Kubernetes.

Real-time vehicle towing and logistics dispatch platform. Decoupled into TypeScript Gateway (auth & aggregation) and a standalone Go Core Engine via gRPC to isolate heavy graph computations. Scaled POI datasets from ~1.5M to ~2.8M records and ~70K administrative boundaries. Replaced slow polygon intersection with Uber H3 hexagonal indexing (O(1) lookup). Tuned OSRM with Multi-Level Dijkstra (MLD) for toll-road calculation.
